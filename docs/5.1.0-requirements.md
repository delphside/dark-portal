# Requirements

What Dark Portal must do, numbered so tests, checks and commits can cite them.
The design documents say how each is met.

> **Draft for #7's design.** Copied from #5, which these replace once merged.

## Scope

Retail World of Warcraft, the EU region: the EU-wide commodities auction house,
and the connected realm holding Turalyon (id 1402). One user, the owner. No
Battle.net player sign-in, no alerts and no in-game add-on in the first
version. The game version is not assumed fixed.

## Requirements

| | Requirement | Stage | Met by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The current auction price of an item can be seen. | 1 | [5.2.3](5.2.3-consumable-profiles.md#the-price-now) |
| R2 | An item's price history can be seen: hourly for 4 weeks, and a daily summary (low, median, volume) kept indefinitely (provisional, to review once the data is understood). | 1 | [5.2.3](5.2.3-consumable-profiles.md#history) |
| R3 | Opportunities to make money on the auction house are identified, distinguishing a one-off mispriced listing from a lasting scheme, with risk shown beside margin. | 2 | |
| R4 | Crafting margin is the product price less the 5% auction cut less the material cost, shown from the lowest price listed now and from a recent typical price (a trimmed, time-weighted median over about 14 days), side by side. Flipping is a recipe A to A. | 3 | |
| R5 | Each item's cost is the lowest of the vendor's price, the auction price and the production price, recursively through the recipes available at the owner's selected profession, profession level and character level; loops are cut. Gathering is shown separately, with its time, and regarded as expensive. | 3 | |
| R6 | Listings below the price a vendor pays for the item are flagged. | 2 | |
| R7 | Commodity depth: the units listed below each price, and turnover per hour as a lower bound. | 1 | [5.2.3](5.2.3-consumable-profiles.md#depth) |
| R8 | Volume, estimated from listings vanishing between snapshots, is shown beside each margin; time is a separate measure, and the headline is profit per hour, read with volume. | volume: 1; the rest: 3 | [5.2.3](5.2.3-consumable-profiles.md#flows) |
| R9 | Gear is priced by comparable group (item level, slot, armour material), with time to sell estimated from history. | 4 | |
| R10 | Cross-market arbitrage across the owner's configured EU connected realms. | 4 | |
