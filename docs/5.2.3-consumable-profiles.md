# Consumable profiles

What Dark Portal shows about one commodity: its price now, its depth, its
flows and its history. Every measure comes from the events in
[5.2.2](5.2.2-listing-lifecycle-and-events.md); this document says what each
measure means and how it is calculated.

> **Draft for #7's design.** The measures marked *(card)* follow the option
> recommended to the owner on 2026-11-16 and change if he chooses another.

## Which items

Every commodity in the EU snapshot has a profile, about 11,700 items *(card)*.
A client filters them by name and by Blizzard's item class and subclass. Each
item's name, class, subclass and vendor prices come from Blizzard's item data,
fetched once for each new item id and landed raw in bronze like the snapshots.

## The price now

**Lowest**: the lowest unit price listed in the latest snapshot.

**Fill price for *N* units**: the average unit price of the cheapest
*N* units listed, which is what buying *N* would cost each. *N* is given when
the profile is asked for, and defaults to one hour's sales, the item's median
hourly sold estimate over the last 7 days, rounded up to a whole unit. Where
fewer than *N* units are listed, the fill price is for all of them and says so.

Both are shown beside the item's **vendor sell price** (what a vendor pays) and,
where a vendor sells it, its **vendor buy price**.

## Depth

The depth ladder (R7) lists each distinct price, from the lowest up, with:

- the units listed at that price;
- the units listed at or below it;
- the hours those units last, the units at or below it divided by the sold
  estimate per hour over the last 24 hours.

The ladder stops at 48 hours of depth, or at the last price, whichever comes
first.

## Flows

For each hour, in units:

- **posted**: units in new listings (EV-1);
- **sold floor**: units from partial sales (EV-2), certain;
- **sold estimate**: the floor plus units in listings gone from the front
  (EV-3), inferred;
- **expired** and **cancelled**: units in unsold listings (EV-5, EV-6).

Each count is a lower bound on what happened: a listing posted and bought
between two snapshots is never seen. The *turnover per hour* of R7 is the sold
estimate, and the sold floor bounds it from below.

## History

**Hourly, for 4 weeks** (R2): for each hour, the lowest price, the fill price
for any *N*, the units listed, the depth ladder and the flows.

**Daily, kept indefinitely** (R2, provisional): for each day, the lowest price
seen in any hour, the median of the hourly fill price at that day's default
*N*, the units posted, and the sold floor and estimate.

A missed hour shows as a gap, never as a zero.

## Clients

The notebook on the owner's machine reads a copy of gold and the reference
tables, and draws the profile as tables and charts (#3). A CLI command on the
host shows the same profile once its use is clear.
