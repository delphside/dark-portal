# Consumable profiles

What Dark Portal shows about one commodity: its price now, its depth, its
flows and its history. Every measure comes from the events in
[5.2.2](5.2.2-listing-lifecycle-and-events.md), and is a view or macro over
gold ([5.4.2](5.4.2-storage-schema.md#views-and-macros)), so every client
shows the same number. The windows and limits named here are in
[5.4.1](5.4.1-configuration.md).

> **Draft for #7's design.**

## Which items

Every commodity in the EU snapshot has a profile, about 10,500 items. A client
filters them by name and by Blizzard's item class and subclass. Each item's
name, class, subclass and vendor prices come from Blizzard's item data
([5.2.1](5.2.1-collection.md#item-data)).

## Flows

For each hour, in units:

- **posted**: units in new listings (EV-1);
- **sold floor**: units from partial sales (EV-2), certain;
- **sold estimate**: the sold floor plus units in listings gone from the front
  (EV-3), inferred;
- **expired** and **cancelled**: units in unsold listings (EV-5, EV-6),
  inferred.

Posted units and the sold floor are lower bounds: a listing posted and bought
between two snapshots is never seen. The sold estimate is the *turnover per
hour* of R7, and the sold floor bounds it from below.

## The price now

**Lowest**: the lowest unit price listed in the latest snapshot.

**Fill price for *N* units**: the average unit price of the cheapest *N* units
listed, which is what buying *N* would cost each. The client gives *N*, or
takes the *default N*: one hour's sales, the item's median hourly sold estimate
over the default *N* window, rounded up, and at least 1. Where fewer than *N*
units are listed, the fill price is for all of them and says how many.

Both are shown beside the item's *vendor sell price*, what a vendor pays for
one, and, where a vendor sells it, its *vendor buy price*.

## Depth

The depth ladder (R7) lists each distinct price, from the lowest up, with:

- the units listed at that price;
- the units listed at or below it;
- the hours those units last: the units at or below it divided by the sold
  estimate per hour over the turnover window. Where the item sold nothing in
  the window, the hours are shown as *no sales seen*.

The ladder stops at the depth ladder cut-off, or at the last price, whichever
comes first.

## History

**Hourly, for the hourly keep** (R2): for each hour, the lowest price, the
fill price for any *N*, the units listed, the depth ladder and the flows.

**Daily, kept indefinitely** (R2): for each day, the lowest price seen in any
hour, the median of the hourly fill prices at that day's default *N*, the
units posted, and the sold floor and estimate.

A missed hour shows as a gap, never as a zero.

## Clients

The notebook on the owner's machine reads the export, loads the views and
macros shipped in it, and draws the profile as tables and charts (#3). A CLI
command in `dp` is designed once the notebook shows which lookups are asked
often.
