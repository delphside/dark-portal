# Consumable profiles

What Dark Portal shows about one commodity: its price now, its depth, its
flows and its history. Every measure comes from the live listings and events
in [5.2.2](5.2.2-listing-lifecycle-and-events.md), and is a view or macro over
gold ([5.4.2](5.4.2-storage-schema.md#views-and-macros)), so every client
shows the same number. The windows and limits named here are in
[5.4.1](5.4.1-configuration.md).

> **Draft for #7's design.**

## Which items

Every commodity in the EU snapshot has a profile: about 10,500 items in
#12's snapshots. A client filters them by name and by Blizzard's item class
and subclass. Each item's name, class, subclass and vendor prices come from
Blizzard's item data ([5.2.1](5.2.1-collection.md#item-data)).

## Flows

For each hour, in units:

- **posted**: units in new listings (EV-1);
- **sold floor**: units from partial sales (EV-2), certain;
- **sold estimate**: the sold floor plus units in listings gone from the front
  (EV-3), inferred;
- **expired** and **cancelled**: units in unsold listings (EV-5, EV-6),
  inferred.

Posted units and the sold floor are lower bounds: a listing posted and bought
between two snapshots is missed. R7's *turnover per hour* is shown as the sold
floor, its lower bound, with the sold estimate beside it.

## The price now

**Lowest**: the lowest unit price listed in the latest snapshot.

**Fill price for *N* units**: the average unit price of the cheapest *N* units
listed, which is what buying *N* would cost each.

- The client gives *N*, or takes the *default N*: one hour's sales, the item's
  median hourly sold estimate over the default *N* window ending at the hour,
  rounded up and at least 1. Where the window holds no hour to count, it is 1.
- A window *ending at* an hour holds that hour and the hours before it, to the
  window's length: 168 hours for 7 days.
- A median or a mean of hourly flows, the default *N* and the depth ladder's
  divisor, counts only the hours held with a span of 1, since an hour after a
  gap holds the flows of several. A total, a day's or the profile's, takes
  every held hour, and so does a day's median fill price.
- Where fewer than *N* units are listed, the fill price is for all of them and
  says how many; where none are, it is null, as is the units it covers.
- An *N* below 1 is refused.
- The fill price is held to two decimal places of copper, rounded half away
  from zero.

Both are shown beside the item's *vendor sell price*, what a vendor pays for
one, and, where a vendor sells it, its *vendor buy price*.

An item's name, class, subclass and vendor prices are read from the body of
its latest 200 response in bronze's item files
([5.4.2](5.4.2-storage-schema.md#reference)). A body that lacks a name, class
or subclass gives null; a missing `sell_price` is 0, and a missing or 0
`purchase_price` is null, since no vendor sells the item. A profile shows the
item's ID as its name until it has a response.

## Depth

The depth ladder (R7) lists each distinct price, from the lowest up, with:

- the units listed at that price;
- the units listed at or below it;
- the hours those units last: the units at or below it divided by the mean
  hourly sold estimate over the hours held in the turnover window
  ([5.2.2](5.2.2-listing-lifecycle-and-events.md#times)). Where the item sold
  nothing in the window, the hours are shown as *no sales seen*.

The ladder stops at the first price whose hours reach the depth ladder
cut-off, which it shows, or at the last price. *No sales seen* is a null in
the hours column.

## History

**Hourly, for the hourly keep** (R2): for each hour, the lowest price, the
fill price for any *N*, the units listed, the depth ladder and the flows.

**Daily, kept indefinitely** (R2): for each day, the lowest price seen in any
hour, the median of the hourly fill prices at the default *N* at 00:00 that
day, the units posted, and the sold floor and estimate. A day is closed once
each of its hours is processed or recorded as a gap; the hours before the
first snapshot collected are not the day's, so the first day closes on the
hours it has. `dp daily` closes every day that can be closed and is not.

A missed hour shows as a gap, never as a zero. An item with no listings in an
hour, but listings the hour before, has a row with no lowest price and its
flows, so a sell-out is kept; the hour after, with nothing listed either time,
has none.

An hour is *held* in gold where any item has a row for it, which is how the
export, which has no `meta`, shows it. An item with no row in a held hour
sold nothing in it, so the medians and means above count that hour as 0.

## Clients

The notebook on the owner's machine reads the export, loads the views and
macros shipped in it, and draws the profile as tables and charts (#3).
